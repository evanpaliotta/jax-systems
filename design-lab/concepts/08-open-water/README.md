## Variant: Open Water

### Design stance
A restrained, expansive coastal direction that uses typography, negative space, and one horizon illustration to feel polished and calm.

### Key choices
- Layout: Full-width typographic hero, wide horizon illustration, and lighter section framing throughout.
- Typography: Large minimalist sans with generous spacing and low visual clutter.
- Color: White, ocean blue, and pale mist for a clean nautical feel without yacht luxury cues.
- Interaction: Industry story switcher, mobile nav toggle, and accessible preview-only fit-call modal.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: Calm confidence, visual breathing room, and premium restraint.
- Weak at: Less overtly explanatory at first glance; depends on type and spacing doing more work.

### Best for
- A brand direction that wants to feel mature, coastal, and highly considered without adding decorative noise.
