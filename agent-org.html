How to use it

Ask Hermes in Telegram with Jax Chief of Staff or a Jax-specific request.

Try: “Jax Chief of Staff: review our current offer and tell me which decisions remain open before launch.”

Or: “Jax: prepare an onboarding and acceptance checklist for our internal operating system.”

Shared knowledge stays in the existing Jax notes and source library. Relevant procedures load on demand; listing a skill is not a claim that its external integration is connected. Missing facts and business decisions stay visible.

Boundaries

  • Internal research, analysis, checklists and local drafts only in v1.
  • No outreach, purchases, signatures, production execution or client-system access.
  • Workers have no shell, browser, email or banking tools; tool actions are not unlocked by a prompt.
  • Private task records and source context are never published here.
  • A separate reviewer and deterministic artifact checks are required; a second opinion is not an audit guarantee.
  • No Anthropic/Claude models, automatic router or silent model fallback.

This is a sanitized capability guide, not a live task dashboard. Real task/client records and model outputs are private. Source inspiration: thin harness, reusable skills; architecture uses Hermes skills, existing approved inference and a bounded local control plane. GStack/GBrain were not installed.
