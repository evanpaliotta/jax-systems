## Variant: Surf Workshop

### Design stance
Bright, bold, and practical—this concept borrows the energy of a local workshop and surf-town signage without tipping into cartoonishness.

### Key choices
- Layout: Chunky bordered panels, stacked hero modules, and graphic shape blocks.
- Typography: Sora for bold headlines with Space Grotesk for a direct working feel.
- Color: Orange, cobalt, cream, and white with thick outlines.
- Interaction: Segmented industry filter, strong CTA blocks, and the shared accessible preview modal.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: Energy, memorability, and fast scan-ability.
- Weak at: Louder than the other concepts; could feel too assertive for a quieter positioning.

### Best for
- A simple/badass direction that still talks like a field operator instead of a startup landing page.
