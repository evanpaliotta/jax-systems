Call captureTrades

The phone rings. You’re under a sink.

See a possible fix

What slipsThe callback starts with a number and half a voicemail.

What could changeCollect the address, problem and callback window. Put one clear summary in the office’s inbox.

Stays humanYour team diagnoses the problem, prices the job and decides when to visit.

Quote follow-upTrades

The estimate went out. Then the day took over.

See a possible fix

What slipsOpen quotes disappear behind today’s urgent work.

What could changeQueue a scoped follow-up, stop on a reply, and bring exceptions back to the estimator.

Stays humanFinal numbers, discounts and anything that needs a conversation.

Customer updatesTrades

The crew knows. The customer doesn’t.

See a possible fix

What slipsOffice staff spend the afternoon relaying routine job updates.

What could changeTurn approved job-status changes into a draft customer update in the tools you use.

Stays humanSomeone checks sensitive details and approves what the customer hears.

What slipsThe maintenance request arrives in pieces across calls and messages.

Stays humanYour team handles emergencies, resident concerns and work authorization.

Internal reportingProperty management

What slipsRoutine status reporting means copying the same facts by hand.

Service intakeMarine services

What slipsRecurring maintenance depends on one person remembering to check.

What could changeQueue reminders from an agreed schedule and make the owner of the next step visible.

Stays humanYour team confirms availability and sets maintenance requirements.
