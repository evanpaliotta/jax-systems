Borrow the principles. Not the costume.

Our earlier research covered seven agency sites. This round revisits useful references and adds adjacent service/design businesses. Appearance does not prove conversion performance.

Goodspeed ↗

Recognizable situations before services; specific packages and visible pricing. Borrow the buyer-language stories and scope clarity—not its client logos, results or scarcity claims.

Main Street Operator ↗

Field-business relevance and a human founder presence. Borrow the respect for people doing the work—not the name, testimonials or operating claims.

Designjoy ↗

A legible service model, straightforward process and practical FAQs. Borrow the clarity—not an unlimited-service promise or a pricing model Jax cannot support.

Instrument ↗

Confident typography, space and work-first presentation. Borrow visual hierarchy—not enterprise positioning or a portfolio we do not have.

Nate Herk’s useful design principles

Brand system before page generation; intentional visitor journey; purposeful motion; screenshot and mobile verification; specific feedback instead of endless “make it better” iterations.

How to review

  • Pick two or three directions, rather than trying to choose a final website immediately.
  • Be specific: “the headline size from 03, the colors from 01, the stories from 05.”
  • Say what feels wrong too: too corporate, too beach rental, too busy, too much software.
  • Use Export feedback and send the JSON back. Notes stay in this browser; they are not automatically sent or synced to us.
