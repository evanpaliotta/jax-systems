## Variant: Straight Answers

### Design stance
A light coastal concept built around trust-through-transparency: clear audience, plain-language pricing, specific operational fixes, and an honest first CTA instead of borrowed proof.

### Source connections
- Uses the Liam Ottley interview with Arthur as structure guidance: clarity in seconds, problem paired with exact solution, easy CTA, simple process, visible pricing, and FAQ-driven objection handling.
- Deliberately avoids copying the video's weaker advice where it would require fabrication here: no invented testimonials, stars, logos, ROI, availability claims, or causality claims tied to someone else's bookings.
- Keeps the business broad across functions and industries, even while using one practical hero example to make the offer concrete.

### Key choices
- Split hero with very large type, one practical client situation, and immediately visible $999 discovery / credit breakdown.
- Problem → specific fix narrative plus a dedicated “what stays yours” ownership section.
- Plain-language pricing for first setup, ongoing care, additional deployments, and exclusions.
- Interactive industry explorer for trades, property management, and marine with three realistic people / stories / fixes in each.
- Preview-only CTA modal that explicitly says it is not connected and has no backend.
- Self-contained HTML with inline CSS, JS, and SVG; no fake photography, no yacht clichés.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: making the offer feel trustworthy, specific, and buyer-friendly for cautious small-business operators.
- Weak at: being less flashy than a more stylized concept and intentionally refusing social-proof shortcuts that can create instant hype.

### Verification focus
- Built to support keyboard focus, responsive behavior, reduced motion, mobile nav toggle, industry switching, FAQ expand/collapse, and preview modal interaction.
