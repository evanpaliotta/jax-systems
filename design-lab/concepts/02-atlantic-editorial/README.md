## Variant: Atlantic Editorial

### Design stance
Oversized serif typography and print-style rules position Jax Systems like a confident local publication rather than a generic agency homepage.

### Key choices
- Layout: Editorial two-column hero, side notes, and ruled sections with a publication rhythm.
- Typography: Newsreader headline system paired with restrained sans body copy.
- Color: Navy and ivory with restrained teal accents.
- Interaction: Industry filter lives in a left rail; the modal keeps the fit-call CTA interactive without pretending booking works.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: Authority, readability, and quiet confidence.
- Weak at: Less playful and less immediately energetic than the bolder workshop direction.

### Best for
- A brand that wants to look considered, informed, and serious without feeling corporate.
