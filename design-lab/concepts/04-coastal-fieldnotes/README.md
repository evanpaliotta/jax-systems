## Variant: Coastal Fieldnotes

### Design stance
Tactile, document-like, and understated—more field guide than marketing page, with numbered notes and coastal contour artwork.

### Key choices
- Layout: Note-sheet hero, numbered sections, and dossier-style entries for delivery and pricing.
- Typography: Literata for a calm editorial tone with neutral sans body copy.
- Color: Pale sand, olive, and ink with low-key contrast between paper surfaces.
- Interaction: Industry filter updates the field-note cards and the fit-call CTA opens the shared preview modal.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: Restraint, trust, and communicating process clearly.
- Weak at: Less immediately flashy; asks the brand to lean into patience and detail.

### Best for
- A concept that should feel grounded, public-safe, and credible for owner-run local businesses across multiple trades.
