## Variant: Clear Harbor

### Design stance
A light, premium coastal direction built around immediate problem-to-fix clarity instead of generic AI positioning. The tone stays beachside and confident, but the language stays plain enough for a busy local operator.

### Key choices
- Layout: Poster-scale centered hero, then an oversized side-by-side "The call you missed" / "A clear next step" comparison before anything process-heavy.
- Visual system: White-sand background, marine teal, navy, and terracotta accents for a polished coastal feel without default black-agency styling.
- Content strategy: Function-first catalog instead of hollow tech feature cards, then an industry switcher to preserve a broader portfolio across trades, property management, and marine services.
- Trust stance: Transparent pricing, direct CTA, honest FAQ, and no fake testimonials, logos, stars, benchmarks, or invented ROI.
- Interaction: Accessible mobile menu, industry switcher, FAQ accordion, and preview-only booking modal with no backend.

### Source connections
- From the Liam Ottley + Arthur discussion: clarity in seconds, problem immediately paired with solution, benefit-led copy, simple process steps, plain CTA, transparent pricing, and FAQ handling objections up front.
- I deliberately did **not** carry over the video's advice about adding generic proof blocks when proof is not real. This concept keeps the structure lessons while refusing invented validation.
- The comparison section directly reflects the transcript's core correction: do not show process before the visitor understands the specific fix.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: Immediate comprehension, honest scope/pricing, premium but readable presentation, and keeping multiple industries visible without becoming vague.
- Weak at: Less flashy than a motion-heavy or dark-theme concept; it depends on copy discipline and hierarchy more than spectacle.

### Best for
- A Jax direction that wants to feel high-trust, expensive in a good way, and operator-first — especially if the brand needs to cover multiple local-business functions without sounding like a generic AI studio.
