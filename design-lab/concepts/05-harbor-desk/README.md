## Variant: Harbor Desk

### Design stance
A bright operator board for owners who want practical structure, transparent pricing, and immediate clarity.

### Key choices
- Layout: Split hero with a large message on the left and an organized "desk board" on the right.
- Typography: Big confident sans-serif display with compact operational labels.
- Color: White, navy, and signal yellow for a clean Jacksonville-coastal feel without generic startup black.
- Interaction: Industry story switcher, real mobile nav toggle, and an accessible preview-only fit-call modal.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: Making the offer easy to scan, showing process clearly, feeling trustworthy and organized.
- Weak at: Less emotionally distinctive than the warmer or more editorial concepts.

### Best for
- Owner-run field or service businesses that respond to practical language, visible process, and clear next steps.
