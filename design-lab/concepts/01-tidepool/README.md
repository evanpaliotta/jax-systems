## Variant: Tidepool

### Design stance
Airy coastal direction with sea-glass tones, soft curves, and a split hero that feels calm without drifting into lifestyle fluff.

### Key choices
- Layout: Asymmetric hero with a large organic art panel and soft glass surfaces below.
- Typography: Fraunces for display warmth, Inter for plain-language body copy.
- Color: Sea-glass, cream, and deep teal with high-contrast text.
- Interaction: Industry filter swaps three real problem stories; fit-call CTA opens an accessible preview modal.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: Approachability, warmth, and making systems work feel less intimidating.
- Weak at: Less sharp if the brand wants a harder-edged or more industrial first impression.

### Best for
- A Jacksonville-facing brand that wants to feel local, calm, and capable for owner-run service businesses.
