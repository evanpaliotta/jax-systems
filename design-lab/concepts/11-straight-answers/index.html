Problem to specific fix

See the problem. See a possible fix.

Here are three everyday situations and practical ways to make the next step easier.

Problem

New work arrives in too many places.

Calls, emails, website forms, resident messages, and text threads all ask for attention first. The team then has to reconstruct the request before any real work begins.

Specific fix

Create one intake layer that cleans and routes the request.

Jax can turn those mixed inputs into one consistent internal summary with the important fields already pulled out: who it is, what happened, what needs to be approved, and who should touch it next.

Problem

Follow-up slips between the field and the office.

A tech finishes the work, a resident is waiting on an update, or a vendor needs a next step. The team knows it matters, but the reminder lives in someone's head or on a whiteboard.

Specific fix

Use one handoff flow that prepares the message before the day moves on.

The system drafts the right update, assembles the job notes, and leaves the actual send or approval to the human who owns the relationship.

Problem

People retype the same details into multiple tools.

One request becomes five small admin jobs. That is expensive, boring, and usually where details go missing.

Specific fix

Let the first action set up the next actions.

Instead of asking the team to copy information between tools, Jax starts with the records you already keep and only automates the handoff points that save real time.

What stays yours

You keep the useful parts.

  • Your business information and existing accounts remain under your control.
  • The proposal will specify which newly created materials you can use and retain.
  • Your domain, accounts, and existing software relationships.
  • Your operating decisions: who approves what, where humans step in, and what should never be automated.
  • Your records and exports from the tools you already pay for, subject to those tools' own limits.

Your business knowledge belongs to you. The point of the first deployment is to leave you with a system and documentation that still make sense when viewed in plain language.

Ongoing

Care, tuning, and additional deployments.

$499/mo first

The first live system starts at $499/month care. Further deployments start at $999 plus $249/month additional care, subject to scope.

  • Care is for upkeep, review, small adjustments, and continuity once the system is live.
  • Additional deployments are for the next useful lane after the first one is working.
  • Scope is judged by the number of people, tools, exceptions, and approval points involved.

Included

What the first engagement is meant to include.

  • Workflow review in plain language.
  • Recommendation for one first system worth implementing.
  • Setup of that first scoped system using existing software first where possible.
  • Basic operating notes so the team understands what it does and where humans stay involved.

Not included unless scoped

What should not be assumed from the base numbers.

  • Paid vendor subscriptions, telephony charges, or software seats.
  • Full re-platforming of the business onto new core software.
  • Custom compliance, legal review, or policy drafting.
  • Hardware, ad spend, content production, or a broad website rebuild.
