## Variant: Sunrise Studio

### Design stance
A warmer, more personal direction that frames Jax Systems as a premium but grounded service for owner-led businesses.

### Key choices
- Layout: Spacious two-column hero with an original sunrise illustration and softer rounded sections.
- Typography: Mixed serif headline and sans body to feel elegant without losing readability.
- Color: Coral, peach, sand, and deep brown for a coastal sunrise palette.
- Interaction: Industry story switcher, mobile nav toggle, and accessible preview-only fit-call modal.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: Human warmth, perceived personal attention, premium-but-friendly tone.
- Weak at: Less overtly hard-edged or operational than Harbor Desk or Local Current.

### Best for
- Founder-led businesses that buy with both trust and logic, especially when a friendlier presentation helps the work feel approachable.
