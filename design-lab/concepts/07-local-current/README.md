## Variant: Local Current

### Design stance
A bold modern local-paper/poster direction with loud hierarchy, hard bands, and direct field-business language.

### Key choices
- Layout: Strong horizontal bands, poster blocks, and a split newsroom-style hero.
- Typography: Heavy uppercase sans hierarchy for instant scan and attitude.
- Color: Electric ultramarine, off-white, and lime accent; intentionally avoids default black-agency styling.
- Interaction: Industry story switcher, mobile nav toggle, and accessible preview-only fit-call modal.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: Standing out fast, feeling contemporary and local, making content impossible to miss.
- Weak at: The most assertive concept; could feel too loud for a softer service posture.

### Best for
- A brand that wants to feel sharp, visible, and unmistakably different from typical AI agency pages.
