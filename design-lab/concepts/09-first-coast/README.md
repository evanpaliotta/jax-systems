## Variant: First Coast

### Design stance
A bright Jacksonville-coastal direction that opens with one concrete missed-call story, then broadens into a portfolio-friendly local-service homepage without pretending to be a narrow single-offer site.

### Key choices
- Layout: Editorial split hero with a missed-call story on the left and an illustrative what-happens-next panel on the right.
- Color: Warm ivory base, confident navy, teal support tones, and restrained orange accents.
- Messaging: Immediate audience/value clarity, problem directly paired with a specific fix, outcome-first copy, transparent pricing, and explicit human-overview boundaries.
- Interaction: Mobile menu, industry explorer tabs for Trades / Property management / Marine, FAQ accordion, and an accessible preview-only fit-call modal.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: Clarity, honesty, local-service relevance, and feeling distinct from dark SaaS agency pages.
- Weak at: Less dramatic than a harder-edged poster concept and intentionally avoids flashy proof devices because none are validated.

### Best for
- A homepage direction that needs one strong leading story while still supporting a broader Jacksonville service-business portfolio.
